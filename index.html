Studio policies

How we keep appointments smooth

Deposit

$50 holds your appointment and is applied to your service total. Deposits are non-refundable but transferable once with 48 hours’ notice.

Cancellation

Please cancel or reschedule at least 48 hours ahead. Late cancellations forfeit the deposit. No-shows may be charged 50% of the booked service.

Late arrival

A 15-minute grace period applies. After that, your service may be shortened or rescheduled so we stay on time for the next guest.

FAQ

Before you book

Do I need to bring hair?

For braiding services, yes — or request a hair add-on during booking. Wig installs require your unit clean and tangle-free.

How does the deposit work on a real site?

In a live Booked Beauty System setup, clients pay the deposit online at booking. This demo only simulates the confirmation screen.

Can I book for kids?

Yes for ages 8+ on select braid services. Please note age in the booking notes on a live site so timing can be adjusted.

What if I’m running late?

Text the studio. After 15 minutes we may need to shorten the service or move you to the next open slot.

Is this a real salon?

No — Luxe Strands Studio is a fictional brand built as a sales demo for The Booked Beauty System.
