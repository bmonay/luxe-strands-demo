Booking Policies

PAYMENT

A $50 deposit holds your chair and goes toward the service. The rest is due at your appointment.

LATE POLICY

A 15 minute grace period is given. After that the service may be shortened or the chair released.

REFUNDS

Deposits are non-refundable. A no-show releases the chair and the deposit stays with the booking.

CANCELLATION

48 hour notice is required to cancel or reschedule so the slot can be filled.

Prepare for appointment

DEPOSIT POLICY

Make sure your $50 deposit is paid to secure the slot. Bookings without a deposit are not confirmed.

BOOKING CONFIRMATION

Please confirm your appointment time ahead of the visit. Unconfirmed slots may be released.

HAIR PREP

Arrive with clean, dry hair. No oils, product, or buildup, please.

GUESTS POLICY

No extra guests during your appointment. Please come alone so the session stays on time.

PUNCTUALITY

Please arrive on time. Late arrivals may shorten the service or move the booking.

DRESS CODE

Wear a comfy top that is easy to change in and out of.
